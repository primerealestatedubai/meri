# MERI walkthrough

Video walkthrough of the MERI studio. Scrolling takes the visitor from the arrival up the stairs to the front desk, and stops there. The first screen also has a Click to continue button that scrolls there by itself with the same motion (`playIn`, about 4 seconds; any wheel, touch or key press stops it). From the front desk, icons lead to the rest: the Memberships icon (box and cards), the stone icon (Yoga Studio and SculptFormer, with an icon on each door) and the fire and water icon (cold plunge and sauna, each with an icon that opens a photo gallery). The old full-length scroll (sauna, yoga, booking end screen) was cut from `STEPS`; the booking end screen is currently unreachable.

- `index.html`: the standalone page. Open it from any static host (`python3 -m http.server`).
- `artifact.html`: the same page as a fragment, used for the Claude artifact preview.
  Keep it in sync with `index.html`.
- `assets/tour.mp4`: the walkthrough, rebuilt from the final stairs, front desk and hallway images (17.5 s, 419 frames, 1920x1080, keyframe every 6 frames for scrubbing): stairs landing to the front desk (8 s, with extra motion blur on the left and right wall and a stray left-edge sliver covered), front desk to hallway (the darker second take), and hallway through the glass door (it opens away from the camera, to the right) into the sauna and cold plunge room. The clips are joined with 8-frame dissolves; the time marks are `T` in the script (`stairs` 3.0, `desk` 185/24, `hall` 298/24, `spa` 418/24). The first frame is the new stairs image, which is also the first screen.
- `assets/tour2.mp4`: the second video (14.1 s, 339 frames, 1920x1080, three stretches; the time marks are `T2` in the script): a 3-second pan to the left from the front desk to the new Move area entrance (0 to `yogaIn`, played at `MOVERATE`), then through the angled glass door into the yoga room (a short dissolve from the entrance frame, up to `yoga`), and, as a separate clip that also starts on the Move area frame, through the left doorway into the SculptFormer room (`pil0` to `pil1`). All three were made from the final Move area frame; the two room clips end on the same room frames as before so the room boxes line up.
- `assets/box.mp4`: front desk, camera in to the box, lid opens (1080p, 5s; regenerated from the front desk frame to the open stone box with the engraved MERI, then Topaz-upscaled). In the script, `CARDS_AT` (4.0 s, when the lid is fully open) and `TRAY` (centre of the open tray in the last frame) tell the page when and where the cards rise. Played fast from the Memberships icon on the front desk; the three membership cards rise out of the open box. The cards are live HTML, not pictures (`#mem .card` in the page): a photo (`assets/mc-essential.webp`, `mc-balance.webp`, `mc-unlimited.webp`, cropped from the design mockup, so low resolution; replace with the originals when available) that fades into a cream card, with two faces that cross-fade. A separate tab switcher above the cards (`.mem-tabs`, `setMemView` in the script) flips every card between Memberships (price, sessions, checkout button) and Benefits (a six-row table; the first card carries the row labels). Everything is sized from `--u`, one pixel of the 389 px wide design card, so it scales with `--cw`. The checkout links stay on `a.book` inside `#mem` (the booking script finds them by that selector and the subscription id in the href): Essential 61566, Balance 61591, Unlimited 61592. The Balance card carries the Most popular tag. In `artifact.html` the links are `#`. Closing plays it in reverse.
- `assets/ic-stone.png`, `ic-fire.png`, `ic-water.png`: the three room icons (cream on transparent). On the front desk, the stone icon opens the Yoga and Reformer journey and the fire and water icon opens Sauna and Cold plunge. Each click plays the footage at a fixed speed and stops in the room, with a Back button that plays it in reverse. `JRATE` (3.9x) is the speed of every journey. Inside the rooms the icons sit on the pools, the sauna door and the two doors (`SPOTPT` in the script).
- `assets/g-plunge-*.webp`, `g-sauna-1.webp`: placeholder photos for the two galleries (`GAL` in the script). Replace the files or edit the lists.
- `assets/idle.mp4`: looping idle motion behind the intro blur.
- **Scrolling:** the first screen cannot be scrolled forward (wheel, touch and keys do nothing); only the Click to continue pill moves on, playing the move to the front desk by itself in about 6 s (`playIn`, the `6000`). Once at the front desk, scrolling up (wheel, swipe, Page Up or the up arrow) scrubs back towards the first screen, and scrolling down returns to the desk (`nudgeScroll`). At the top, scrolling down does nothing again. Scrolling is ignored while a pop-up is open, and starting a journey from a pill snaps back to the desk first.

Front desk labels and the sauna and cold plunge flow (latest changes):

- The Memberships, Move, Recovery and Fika Cafe labels show all the time (no hover). Recovery has glowing down arrows, Move and Fika Cafe have glowing left arrows. `assets/ic-cafe.svg` is the coffee mug icon. The Fika Cafe icon only shows a "coming soon" note until its clip is approved.

- The yoga and SculptFormer rooms show no title text. A stone icon near the middle of the screen opens the same pop-up (`SVC.yoga`, `SVC.pilates`; photos `assets/g-yoga-*.webp`, `g-pil-*.webp`, placeholders). The desk title has no kicker line.
