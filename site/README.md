# MERI walkthrough

Video walkthrough of the MERI studio. Scrolling takes the visitor from the arrival up the stairs to the front desk, and stops there. The first screen also has a Click to continue button that scrolls there by itself with the same motion (`playIn`, about 4 seconds; any wheel, touch or key press stops it). From the front desk, icons lead to the rest: the Memberships icon (box and cards), the stone icon (Yoga Studio and SculptFormer, with an icon on each door) and the fire and water icon (cold plunge and sauna, each with an icon that opens a photo gallery). The old full-length scroll (sauna, yoga, booking end screen) was cut from `STEPS`; the booking end screen is currently unreachable.

- `index.html`: the standalone page. Open it from any static host (`python3 -m http.server`).
- `artifact.html`: the same page as a fragment, used for the Claude artifact preview.
  Keep it in sync with `index.html`.
- `assets/tour.mp4`: the walkthrough (17.5 s, 419 frames, 1920x1080, keyframe every 6 frames for scrubbing): from the final stairs landing (also the first screen) down to the front desk (8 s, with strong motion blur on the left and a short blur over a stray wall lamp on the right), front desk to the hallway (the darker take), and hallway through the glass door (it opens away from the camera, to the right) into the sauna and cold plunge room. Joined with 8-frame dissolves; the time marks are `T` in the script (`stairs` 3.0, `desk` 185/24, `hall` 298/24, `spa` 418/24). `assets/idle.mp4` is the loop behind the first screen and `assets/poster.jpg` is its first frame.
- `assets/tour2.mp4`: the second video (18 s, 433 frames, three stretches; the time marks are `T2` in the script): a 3-second pan to the left from the front desk to the new Move area entrance (0 to `yogaIn`, played at `MOVERATE`), then a short dissolve into the earlier clips, through the glass door into the yoga room (to `yoga`) and the mirror door into the SculptFormer room (`pil0` to `pil1`). Until new yoga and SculptFormer clips are made from the new Move area frame, those two stretches still start from the old entrance look, so there is a visible change when entering them. The previous version is kept out of the repo.
- `assets/box.mp4`: front desk, camera in to the box, lid opens (1080p, 5s; regenerated from the front desk frame to the open stone box with the engraved MERI, then Topaz-upscaled). In the script, `CARDS_AT` (4.0 s, when the lid is fully open) and `TRAY` (centre of the open tray in the last frame) tell the page when and where the cards rise. Played fast from the Memberships icon on the front desk; the three membership cards (`assets/m1-essential.webp`, `m2-balance2.webp`, `m3-unlimited.webp`) rise out of the open box. Closing plays it in reverse.
- `assets/ic-stone.png`, `ic-fire.png`, `ic-water.png`: the three room icons (cream on transparent). On the front desk, the stone icon opens the Yoga and Reformer journey and the fire and water icon opens Sauna and Cold plunge. Each click plays the footage at a fixed speed and stops in the room, with a Back button that plays it in reverse. `JRATE` (3.9x) is the speed of every journey. Inside the rooms the icons sit on the pools, the sauna door and the two doors (`SPOTPT` in the script).
- `assets/g-plunge-*.webp`, `g-sauna-1.webp`: placeholder photos for the two galleries (`GAL` in the script). Replace the files or edit the lists.
- `assets/idle.mp4`: looping idle motion behind the intro blur.
- **No page scrolling:** the page cannot be scrolled by wheel, touch or keys, and the scroll dots are hidden. The first screen has only the Click to continue pill, which plays the move to the front desk by itself in about 6 s (`playIn`, the duration is the `6000` in that function). The scroll machinery still drives the video underneath, but only the script moves it.

Front desk labels and the sauna and cold plunge flow (latest changes):

- The Memberships, Move, Recovery and Fika Cafe labels show all the time (no hover). Recovery has glowing down arrows, Move and Fika Cafe have glowing left arrows. `assets/ic-cafe.svg` is the coffee mug icon. The Fika Cafe icon only shows a "coming soon" note until its clip is approved.
- The top bar has Studio map and Book a session (Memberships lives on the desk). Studio map opens a full-screen floor plan (`assets/studio-plan.webp`, outlines in the `ROOMS` list of the map script at the end of the page). Hovering a room lifts it off the plan, dims the rest and shows its name and photo; clicking opens its photos (`FRAMES`). Rooms without photos (Fika Cafe, Washrooms and showers) say photos are coming soon. Esc or the round close button closes it.
- The Recovery pill plays the hallway to the lockers, holds half a second, then glides on slowly (`SPARATE`) into the sauna and cold plunge room with no scrolling. The earlier scroll-through-the-door code (`hall` state) is still in the script but is no longer entered. In that room a Move pill goes back to the desk and on to the yoga entrance.
- The water and fire icons in the room open a pop-up (`SVC` in the script): photos on the left, benefits and a Book a session button on the right. Placeholder copy and photos.
- The yoga and SculptFormer rooms show no title text. A stone icon near the middle of the screen opens the same pop-up (`SVC.yoga`, `SVC.pilates`; photos `assets/g-yoga-*.webp`, `g-pil-*.webp`, placeholders). The desk title has no kicker line.
